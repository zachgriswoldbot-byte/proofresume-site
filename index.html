How it works

The job search starts with one thing you already have: your resume.

1. Upload your resume

Import your resume once. ProofResume extracts roles, skills, proof points, gaps, and preferences.

2. Source and rank jobs

Find relevant roles, score fit, flag weak matches, and prioritize applications worth your time.

3. Tailor every application

Generate role-specific resume bullets, cover notes, and application answers without inventing claims.

4. Approve before apply

Review the proof, edit the final packet, and decide what can be submitted or skipped.

Why it is different

Auto-apply speed with a proof and approval layer.

  1. 01No resume fan fiction

    Every stronger claim needs a source in your resume, notes, or approved answers.

  2. 02Fewer bad applications

    Fit scoring shows which jobs are worth tailoring and which ones are likely a waste.

  3. 03You stay in control

    ProofResume can prepare the work, but approval gates prevent surprise sends.

  4. 04A search you can manage

    Track sourced, tailored, ready, applied, interviewing, rejected, and accepted roles.
